Roofing in Washington DC

Most DC roofing work is low-slope rowhouse roofing, not pitched shingle. That changes everything about the job: access is through the house or off an alley, there is no driveway for a dumpster, and the roof is often directly over finished living space with no attic buffer. We work rowhouses routinely and plan the logistics before the crew shows up.

Permits and local requirements

Historic districts including Capitol Hill, Georgetown, and Mount Pleasant have review requirements for visible roofing materials. We handle that documentation.
