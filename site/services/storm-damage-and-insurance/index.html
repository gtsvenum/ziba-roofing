Storm Damage Repair and Insurance Claim Support

Most storm-damage claims are denied or underpaid because the damage was documented poorly, not because it was not covered — so the photo and measurement record matters as much as the repair.

Call (301) 366-8210 Free inspection

What we document

Dated photographs of every damaged slope, measured hail-strike density per test square, wind-lift and crease evidence on shingle tabs, collateral damage to soft metals like gutters and vents, and interior water intrusion. That record is what an adjuster needs to approve a slope rather than a patch.

We meet the adjuster on site

An adjuster working alone on an unfamiliar roof can miss damage on the back slope. We walk it with them so the scope reflects what is actually up there.

What we will not do

We will not inflate a scope, waive or absorb your deductible, or tell you a roof is destroyed when it is not. Deductible rebating is insurance fraud in Maryland, Virginia, and the District, and any contractor offering it is telling you how they do business.

Storm Damage & Insurance Claims — questions we get

Does homeowners insurance cover roof damage?

Sudden damage from wind, hail, or a fallen tree is generally covered. Gradual wear, age, and deferred maintenance are not. The distinction is why documentation of the storm event matters.

How long do I have to file a roof claim after a storm?

Most policies require prompt notice and many set a one-year limit from the date of loss. Check your specific policy and file as soon as you suspect damage.

Should I use the contractor my insurance company recommends?

You are not required to. You can choose any licensed contractor. A preferred-vendor program is a cost-control arrangement for the insurer, not an endorsement of quality.
