Roof Replacement in the DMV

A full roof replacement in the Washington DC metro area typically takes one to three days and starts with a complete tear-off down to the deck, so rotted sheathing is found and replaced rather than covered up.

Call (301) 366-8210 Free inspection

What a replacement actually includes

Tear-off of all existing layers, inspection and replacement of damaged decking, synthetic underlayment, ice-and-water shield at eaves and valleys, new drip edge, new pipe boots and flashing, ridge ventilation, and the finish roofing material. Flashing and boots are where most roofs fail first, so we replace them every time rather than reusing them.

How long it takes

Most DC rowhouse and suburban Maryland or Virginia roofs are completed in one to three working days. Slate, tile, and large or steep-pitch roofs take longer.

How we price it

Roofing is priced by the square (100 square feet of actual roof surface, not floor area), adjusted for pitch, number of layers to remove, decking condition, and the complexity of valleys, dormers, chimneys, and skylights. You get a written line-item estimate before any work is scheduled.

Permits and inspections

Like-for-like residential reroofing generally does not require a permit in the District, but structural decking work, additions to the roof structure, and work in a historic district often do. We pull what the jurisdiction requires and schedule the inspections.

Roof Replacement — questions we get

How long does a roof replacement take?

One to three working days for a typical DMV home. Slate, tile, and complex multi-pitch roofs take longer.

Can you install a new roof over the old one?

We do not recommend it and we do not do it. A layover hides rotted decking, voids most manufacturer warranties beyond the first layer, adds dead load, and shortens the life of the new shingles because of trapped heat.

How much does a new roof cost in Washington DC?

Most DMV residential replacements fall between $8,000 and $30,000 depending on squares, pitch, material, and decking repairs. Flat and metal roofs sit at the upper end. We give a written line-item price before scheduling.
