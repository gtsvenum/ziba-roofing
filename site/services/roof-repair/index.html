Roof Repair and Leak Diagnosis

Most roof leaks are not holes in the field of the roof — they are failed flashing, cracked pipe boots, backed-up valleys, or clogged gutters, and water usually enters well uphill of the ceiling stain you can see.

Call (301) 366-8210 Free inspection

Why the stain is not the leak

Water runs along rafters and sheathing before it drops. A stain over the dining room can originate at a chimney eight feet away. We trace the path from inside the attic or ceiling cavity before we go on the roof, which is why we find leaks that have already been 'fixed' twice.

The five failures we find most

Cracked rubber pipe boots (the single most common), step flashing at wall intersections, chimney counterflashing that was caulked instead of cut in, nail pops under shingle tabs, and ice-dam backup at eaves on north-facing slopes.

Repair or replace

If the roof is under roughly 15 years old and the failure is localized, repair is the right call and we will say so. If the shingles are curling, granules are filling the gutters, and there are multiple leak points, repairs are money spent twice. We tell you which one you are looking at in writing.

Emergency response

We tarp active leaks to stop interior damage, then return for the permanent repair once the weather clears.

Roof Repair & Leak Diagnosis — questions we get

How do I find where my roof is leaking?

Start in the attic during or right after rain and look uphill from the wet spot. Water travels down rafters before it drops, so the entry point is almost always higher on the slope than the interior stain.

Do you do emergency roof tarping?

Yes. We tarp active leaks to stop interior damage and schedule the permanent repair separately.

Is it worth repairing an old roof?

If the roof is under about 15 years old and the failure is localized, repair is usually the better value. Multiple leak points plus curling shingles and granule loss in the gutters means repairs will not hold.
