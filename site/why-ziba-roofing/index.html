Why hire Ziba Roofing

Ziba Roofing is the roofing arm of Ziba Management Inc., a licensed general contractor that builds and renovates whole houses across the DMV — so the person pricing your roof knows how it gets built, not just how to sell it.

Licensed and insured in all three jurisdictions

Ziba Roofing operates under Ziba Management Inc., District of Columbia (DCRA/DLCP) Home Improvement Contractor license #410520000203, carrying general liability and workers' compensation. Ask any roofer for their number and verify it before you sign. Including us.

We are contractors, not a sales organization

The person who prices your roof understands how it gets built, because the same company builds whole houses. You will not get a commissioned closer with a tablet and a discount that expires tonight.

Written line-item estimates

You see the squares, the material, the decking allowance, the flashing scope, and the disposal cost as separate lines. A single lump-sum number is how surprises get hidden.

We replace flashing every time

Flashing and pipe boots are the most common point of failure on a roof and the cheapest thing to reuse if you are cutting corners. We replace them on every full roof. No exceptions.

Clean site, magnet swept

Dumpster placed to protect your driveway and landscaping, tarps over plantings, and a magnetic sweep of the lawn and street at the end of every working day. Roofing nails in a tire are the most common complaint about roofers, and they are entirely preventable.

Straight answers about repair versus replacement

If your roof needs a $600 repair instead of a $20,000 replacement, that is what the estimate will say. Telling a homeowner to replace a sound roof is the fastest way to lose the referrals that actually sustain this business.

How a Ziba roof job runs

  1. Inspection

    We photograph the roof, the flashing details, the attic where accessible, and the gutters. You get the photos whether or not you hire us.

  2. Written estimate

    A line-item scope and price, including a stated allowance for decking replacement so rotted sheathing does not become a surprise invoice.

  3. Scheduling and permits

    We pull any required permit, order material, and give you a start date with a weather contingency.

  4. Installation

    Tear-off, deck inspection, underlayment, flashing, finish material, ventilation. Site protected and swept daily.

  5. Final walkthrough

    We walk the property with you, show you photos of the completed details you cannot see from the ground, and hand over warranty documentation.
