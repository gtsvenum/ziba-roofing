Roofing questions, answered

Straight answers about cost, timing, materials, and insurance for roofs in the Washington DC metro area.

General roofing questions

What areas do you serve?

Ziba Roofing serves the DMV: Washington DC, Montgomery County and Prince George's County in Maryland, and Arlington, Alexandria, and Fairfax County in Virginia.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Ziba Roofing operates under Ziba Management Inc. under District of Columbia (DCRA/DLCP) Home Improvement Contractor license #410520000203, with general liability and workers' compensation coverage. Certificates are available on request.

How much does a new roof cost in the DMV?

Most residential replacements in the Washington DC metro area run between $8,000 and $30,000. The drivers are roof size in squares, pitch, material, how many existing layers come off, and how much decking needs replacing. Flat and metal roofs sit at the upper end.

How do I know if I need a new roof or just a repair?

Shingles curling at the edges, bare patches where granules have washed off, granules collecting in the gutters, daylight visible through the attic sheathing, and leaks in more than one location all point to replacement. A single leak on a roof under 15 years old is usually a repair.

How long does a roof last in the Washington DC area?

Architectural asphalt shingles last 20 to 30 years here, flat membrane roofs 20 to 30 years, standing seam metal 40 to 70, and natural slate 75 to 150. DMV summers are hard on asphalt, so roofs on unventilated attics tend toward the low end of those ranges.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Roof inspection and written estimate are free, and you get the inspection photos whether or not you hire us.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. We document storm damage to the standard an adjuster needs, and we meet the adjuster on site. We do not waive deductibles, which is illegal in DC, Maryland, and Virginia.

What is the best time of year to replace a roof in the DMV?

Late spring through early fall. Asphalt shingles need temperatures above roughly 40°F to seal properly. We install year-round using hand-sealing in cold weather, but given the choice, schedule in mild weather.

How long does a roof replacement take?

One to three working days for most DMV homes. Slate, tile, and large complex roofs take longer.

Do you require a deposit?

A material deposit is due at scheduling with the balance on completion. We do not ask for full payment up front — no legitimate roofer does.
